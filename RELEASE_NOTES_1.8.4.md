# Modulez Player 1.8.4

- Play DOS AdLib music through AdPlug, including Reality AdLib Tracker, HSC, Creative Music File, EdLib and AdLib Tracker II. AdLib tracks use the existing EQ, visualizer and export controls.
- Open more music directly from Modland. The playback routing lists recognize 360 additional distinct filename extensions and aliases, including Art & Magic, Nintendo SPC, S98, Atari SAP and AY Emul.
- Reality AdLib Tracker, Art & Magic and Nintendo SPC playback were confirmed in the Linux Mint desktop build.

## Known limitations

The miniPSF family of tracks that depends on external library files still needs companion-file support. One tested AdLib Tracker II file, `frenzy!!!.a2m`, crashed the AdPlug decoder. Individual songs may vary.
