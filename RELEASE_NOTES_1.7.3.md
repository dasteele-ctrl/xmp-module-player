# Modulez Player 1.7.3

- Double-click a sample in the Samples tab, or select it and click **Play Sample**, to hear the displayed waveform. Click **Stop Preview** to end it early.
- Open **Pattern**, between **Samples** and **SF2/SF3**, to browse individual patterns in tracker modules. Play a pattern once or enable **Loop** to repeat it. The grid uses double-sized text and follows the active row.
- Previewing temporarily suspends song playback. When the preview ends, a playing song resumes from its previous position. A paused or stopped song stays there.
