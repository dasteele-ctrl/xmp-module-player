## The RMF Update

- Play local Beatnik RMF files directly with NeoBAE and the bundled Beatnik Standard v1.1.1 Uncompressed bank.
- Browse the WebTV music collection and play or queue individual tracks.
- Search SoundMusicSys RMF folders by song or collection name.
- Open Headspace album ZIPs inside Browse Music. Play a track from temporary storage, keep a track in a saved playlist, or save the original ZIP to Downloads.
- See download and save progress beside the Headspace link you clicked.
