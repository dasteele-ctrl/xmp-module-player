# Modulez Player 1.8.6

This maintenance release improves local video playback controls.

- Press **Space** in the video window to pause or resume.
- Press **Left** or **Right** to seek backward or forward by five seconds.
- Enter fullscreen by double-clicking the picture, pressing **F11**, or using the fullscreen button. The toolbar floats over the picture and fades after five seconds of inactivity. Move the pointer to reveal it, or press **Esc** to return to the windowed toolbar.
